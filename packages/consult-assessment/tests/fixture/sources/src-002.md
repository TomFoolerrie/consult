# Interview - Controller
Q: How are manual JEs approved?
A: I post them. There is no one else to approve; the CFO looks at the P&L at month end.
Q: Agent commission clawbacks?
A: We track them in a spreadsheet. It is usually right but nobody reconciles it to the GL.
Q: What works well?
A: Bank recs are done within three days, every month, with the CFO signing off.
