NOTES FROM INVOICE SAMPLE (20 invoices, June, pulled by Marcus)

- 14 of 20 PO-backed invoices had a completed three-way match on file.
- 6 of 20 were missing the receipt leg; 4 of those were paid anyway,
  marked "expedite - DO approval" in the memo field.
- Average approval time under $10k: 1.8 days. $10k and above: 6.4 days,
  all waiting on a single approver.
- Two invoices from vendor Kessler Tooling appear twice in the ledger
  with different invoice numbers but identical amounts and dates.
