MERIDIAN MANUFACTURING — ACCOUNTS PAYABLE POLICY (rev 2024-03)

1. All invoices arrive at ap@meridian.example and are logged by the AP
   clerk within one business day.
2. Invoices under $10,000: approved by the requesting department's team
   lead.
3. Invoices of $10,000 and above: require approval by the Controller,
   Dana Okafor.
4. A three-way match (PO, receipt, invoice) is required before payment
   for all PO-backed purchases.
5. Payment runs occur every Thursday.
6. Dana Okafor reports to the CFO (Priya Sharma).
