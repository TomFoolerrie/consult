# objective — Brightline Logistics, employee expense reimbursement

Brightline (a regional freight broker, ~180 staff) asked us to look at how
employee expenses get submitted, approved, and paid. Our contact is Priya
Natarajan (Controller). The relationship is producing: a clear picture of
the current process, where it leaks (policy violations, duplicates, slow
approvals), and a short findings report she can take to the CFO. She has
sent a policy document, a Q2 export from the expense system, and a
forwarded email thread about approvals. Priya is responsive on process
questions and slow on anything touching the corporate card program (that
sits with Treasury, not her).
