# Brightline Logistics — Travel & Expense Policy (rev 4, effective 2025-01-01)

1. Employees submit expenses in Expensify within 30 days of the expense date.
2. Receipts are required for any single expense of $25 or more.
3. Meals are reimbursable up to $60 per day; alcohol is not reimbursable.
4. Approval: the employee's direct manager approves reports up to $1,000.
   Reports over $1,000 require a second approval from the department VP.
5. Mileage is reimbursed at the IRS standard rate.
6. Approved reports are paid in the next weekly AP run (Fridays).
7. Corporate card transactions are reconciled monthly by Treasury; personal
   expenses on the corporate card must be repaid within 30 days.
