# Memo — FX Exposure & Hedging Approach (FY27 Planning)

**From:** Treasury Analyst (contractor, reports to Emmett Suzuki)
**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Re:** Foreign-currency exposure from the CAD steel contracts and the
proposed forward-cover program.

Summary for the planning committee:

1. Roughly 30% of raw-material spend is CAD-denominated (the two Plant 3
   steel suppliers and one Plant 2 supplier). Invoices are payable in CAD;
   we currently buy spot at each payment run and eat the volatility.

2. Proposal: a rolling three-month forward-cover program, sized at 80% of
   forecast CAD payables, executed monthly with the bank's trading desk
   under the existing ISDA. Estimated reduction in quarter-over-quarter
   cost variance: 60–70% based on FY26 rates.

3. Hedge accounting: we would NOT designate under ASC 815 in year one —
   forwards marked to market through the P&L, revisit designation once
   the program is routine. Delphine has seen a draft of this memo.

4. Authority: the program needs a board-level treasury policy amendment
   (current policy is silent on derivatives). Emmett will bring it to the
   October meeting.

Not covered here: the CAD invoice settlement mechanics inside AP (no
change proposed — payment runs continue exactly as today; only the
currency purchase moves from spot to forward delivery).

— for the planning file. Questions to Emmett's office.
