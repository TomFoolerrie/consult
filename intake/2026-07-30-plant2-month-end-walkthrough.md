# Plant 2 Month-End Walkthrough — Receiving Dock to Reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-07-30 · **Present:** Hal (Receiving Supervisor, first
half), the Plant Controller (second half), Warehouse Manager (joined for
the cutoff discussion), consultant. Walked the physical flow from the dock
through to the month-end inventory reconciliation workpaper.

## 1. Dock, morning — the bill of lading question closed

Hal, at the scanning station:

> "You asked last time where the scanned BOL goes. I asked Gideon.
> The scan attaches to the item receipt record in NetSuite — the File
> Cabinet, under the receipt transaction itself. Retention follows the
> transaction: seven years. The paper copy goes in the banker's box under
> the counter and honestly nobody has ever opened one."

So the annotated bill of lading IS retained, electronically, attached to
the receipt record; the earlier uncertainty was only that Hal had never
needed to retrieve one.

## 2. The receiving cutoff — hard rule, stated by three people

Warehouse Manager: "Nothing gets receipted after **noon on the last
business day of the month**. Trucks that arrive after noon wait in the
yard and receipt on the first. That's the rule the whole close hangs on."

Plant Controller, separately, same number: "Noon, last business day. AP's
invoice cutoff is different — they process through the second working day
of the new month — and Corinne holds the GR/IR accrual open until AP's
cutoff passes. The three dates move together; when someone quotes one of
them wrong, the reconciliation is wrong."

(Consultant note: that is the third time this month a cutoff date has been
quoted from memory by someone outside the owning team — these dates want a
single shared home.)

## 3. The month-end inventory reconciliation — where receipts land

The Plant Controller walked the July workpaper:

> "The reconciliation starts from the perpetual balance, and the biggest
> reconciling class every month is receipts-in-transit: item receipts the
> dock posted in the last two days that haven't been billed yet. I pull
> the item receipt register — the SAME postings Hal's team creates at the
> dock — and tie it to the GR/IR account. If receiving posts a receipt
> late, or against the wrong PO line, it surfaces HERE, three weeks later,
> as my reconciling item. My workpaper is downstream of their scan gun."

She was explicit about the dependency direction: the reconciliation
consumes the goods-receipt postings as its input; nothing she does feeds
back into receiving except an error report when a posting is wrong.

Plant 3 caveat (matches the Buyer's account): supplier-owned Kanban bin
stock is excluded from both the counts and the reconciliation — ownership
transfers at backflush consumption. "The exclusion is in my head and in
Yusuf's head. It should be in a procedure."

## 4. The GR/IR accrual — out of the room

The GR/IR accrual journal itself is booked by Delphine's team as part of
the corporate close — the Plant Controller prepares the support, the
entry is record-to-report's. "Where your engagement draws that line is
your business, but the workpaper handoff is real: my reconciliation is
support for a journal I don't book."
