## Key Dependencies

<!-- derived: dependencies; writer: agent -->

The table below reads each procedure's upstream (what must happen or be available before it runs) and downstream (what depends on its output) connections from the procedures' Scope statements. External dependencies — other teams, systems, or activities outside this document's procedures — appear as plain text.

| Procedure | Upstream (depends on) | Downstream (feeds) |
|---|---|---|
| [[new-vendor-onboarding]] | Intake request for a supplier not yet on file; supplier self-registration | [[requisition-and-approval]] (active vendor record enables requisitioning); subsequent remit-to banking changes handled under [[vendor-banking-change]]; ongoing record hygiene under [[vendor-master-data-maintenance]] |
| [[vendor-banking-change]] | Requested change to an existing supplier's remit-to details; initial banking details captured through supplier self-registration in [[new-vendor-onboarding]] | [[weekly-payment-run]], [[wire-and-manual-payment]] (disburse to whatever details this procedure has made active) |
| [[vendor-master-data-maintenance]] | [[new-vendor-onboarding]]; incomplete records arriving from the nightly Coupa-to-NetSuite supplier sync | Invoice matching in [[po-invoice-entry-and-three-way-match]] and every disbursement, including [[weekly-payment-run]], transact against the data this procedure keeps accurate |
| [[requisition-and-approval]] | Active supplier record from [[new-vendor-onboarding]] (a supplier not yet on file must first complete onboarding) | [[po-issuance-and-change-orders]] (fully approved requisition triggers PO generation) |
| [[po-issuance-and-change-orders]] | Fully approved requisition from [[requisition-and-approval]] | [[goods-receipt]] (PO is received against); [[po-invoice-entry-and-three-way-match]] (PO is matched); purchase order terms direct supplier invoices to the AP Inbox for [[invoice-intake-and-capture]] |
| [[confirming-po]] | Off-process purchases that bypass [[requisition-and-approval]]; deliveries that cannot be tied to a purchase order from [[goods-receipt]] | [[po-invoice-entry-and-three-way-match]] (confirming PO enables the returned invoice to be matched and paid) |
| [[goods-receipt]] | Open purchase orders issued through [[po-issuance-and-change-orders]]; physical arrival of goods at the plant receiving docks | [[po-invoice-entry-and-three-way-match]] (item receipt is the receiving leg of the match); [[return-to-vendor]]; [[confirming-po]] (deliveries with no purchase order); month-end received-not-invoiced accrual (Record to Report, outside this process) |
| [[return-to-vendor]] | Goods recorded through [[goods-receipt]] | Expected vendor credit memo offsetting the supplier account, whose invoice activity is processed under [[po-invoice-entry-and-three-way-match]] |
| [[invoice-intake-and-capture]] | Supplier invoices arriving by email or paper mail (suppliers directed to the AP Inbox by PO terms issued in [[po-issuance-and-change-orders]]) | [[po-invoice-entry-and-three-way-match]] (entry and matching); [[non-po-invoice-entry-and-approval]] (coding and approval) — pending bills in NetSuite |
| [[po-invoice-entry-and-three-way-match]] | Pending bill from [[invoice-intake-and-capture]]; approved purchase order from [[po-issuance-and-change-orders]]; item receipt from [[goods-receipt]]; the supplier invoice | [[weekly-payment-run]] (bill eligible for payment); match-exception resolution routed through [[po-issuance-and-change-orders]] (price variances) and [[goods-receipt]] (receipt corrections); [[vendor-statement-reconciliation]] |
| [[non-po-invoice-entry-and-approval]] | Captured bills from [[invoice-intake-and-capture]] | [[weekly-payment-run]] (approved bills enter the weekly disbursement cycle); [[vendor-statement-reconciliation]] |
| [[weekly-payment-run]] | Bills released through [[po-invoice-entry-and-three-way-match]] and approved through [[non-po-invoice-entry-and-approval]]; active remit-to details from [[vendor-banking-change]] | [[positive-pay-exception-handling]] (positive pay issue file transmitted from NetSuite at check print); ACH batch (NACHA file) and check run transmitted to the bank; [[vendor-statement-reconciliation]] |
| [[wire-and-manual-payment]] | Remittance details maintained through [[vendor-banking-change]]; standing wire arrangements for overseas suppliers and the policy-defined manual (emergency) check path | [[positive-pay-exception-handling]] (any check issued feeds the positive pay cycle); monthly bank reconciliation (outside this process); [[vendor-statement-reconciliation]] |
| [[positive-pay-exception-handling]] | Positive pay issue file transmitted at check print and checks issued under [[weekly-payment-run]]; checks issued under [[wire-and-manual-payment]]; exception items flagged by the bank | Pay / return disposition to the bank (undecided items default) |
| [[vendor-statement-reconciliation]] | Supplier statements of account; bills from [[po-invoice-entry-and-three-way-match]] and [[non-po-invoice-entry-and-approval]]; payments from [[weekly-payment-run]] and [[wire-and-manual-payment]] | Completed reconciliation worksheet retained on the Finance Shared Drive |
