## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This Standard Operating Procedure ("SOP") establishes the required methods for the receipt, validation, recording, approval and disbursement of supplier invoices at Nordhaven Industrial Group ("the Company") and at each of its manufacturing facilities.

1.2 This SOP applies to all invoices presented for payment by third-party suppliers of goods and services. This SOP does not apply to payroll disbursements, intercompany settlements, or employee expense reimbursements, which are governed by separate procedures.

1.3 Deviation from this SOP is not permitted absent written authorization from the Corporate Controller. Authorized deviations shall be documented and retained.

---

## 4. INVOICE RECEIPT AND IMAGING

4.1 All supplier invoices shall be directed to the designated accounts payable electronic mailbox. Supplier remittance instructions and purchase order terms and conditions shall state this requirement.

4.2 Invoices received in physical form at the Company's post office box shall be date-stamped upon receipt and scanned to the imaging system not later than one (1) business day following receipt.

4.3 All invoice images, whether received electronically or scanned, shall be processed through the Company's document capture application for extraction of header data. Extracted fields shall include, at minimum: supplier name, supplier invoice number, invoice date, invoice total, currency, and purchase order reference.

4.4 Documents for which the capture application returns an extraction confidence score below ninety percent (90%) shall be routed to the manual validation queue and shall be keyed by an Accounts Payable Specialist.

4.5 The original electronic image shall be retained as an attachment to the resulting payable transaction record for a period of not less than seven (7) years.

---

## 5. INVOICE ENTRY AND MATCHING

5.1 Invoices bearing a valid purchase order reference shall be entered in the enterprise resource planning system by reference to that purchase order. The Accounts Payable Specialist shall not create payable lines manually where a purchase order exists.

5.2 A three-way match shall be performed between the approved purchase order, the recorded goods receipt, and the supplier invoice. All three documents must be present before the payable is eligible for release.

5.3 **Matching tolerance.** A variance between the invoice and the purchase order shall be accepted without further approval where such variance does not exceed five percent (5%) of the line extended value or one hundred dollars ($100.00), whichever is the lesser amount. Variances exceeding this tolerance shall be placed on hold and referred to the responsible Buyer for resolution.

5.4 Quantity variances shall be resolved by reference to the receiving documentation. Where the receiving documentation is unavailable, the Receiving Supervisor at the relevant facility shall be contacted to confirm quantities received. Confirmation shall be obtained in writing.

5.5 Invoices not bearing a purchase order reference shall be entered without purchase order reference and shall be coded to the general ledger by the Accounts Payable Specialist in accordance with the Company chart of accounts. Such invoices shall be routed for approval in accordance with Section 6.

5.6 Duplicate invoice prevention is enforced by the enterprise resource planning system through a unique constraint on the combination of supplier identifier and supplier invoice number. Attempts to enter a duplicate shall be rejected by the system.

---

## 6. APPROVAL AUTHORITY

6.1 Invoices entered without purchase order reference shall be approved in accordance with the following schedule:

| Invoice Value (USD) | Required Approvers |
|---|---|
| Up to $2,500 | Cost Center Owner |
| $2,500.01 to $10,000 | Cost Center Owner; Functional Vice President |
| $10,000.01 to $50,000 | Cost Center Owner; Functional Vice President; Corporate Controller |
| Above $50,000 | Cost Center Owner; Functional Vice President; Corporate Controller; Chief Financial Officer |

6.2 Approvals shall be recorded within the enterprise resource planning system. Approval by electronic mail is not acceptable evidence of approval and shall not be relied upon.

6.3 An approver shall not approve an invoice for which that approver is the requester or the beneficiary.

6.4 Approvals not actioned within three (3) business days shall escalate automatically to the approver's supervisor.

---

## 7. DISBURSEMENT

7.1 The Company shall execute a disbursement cycle once per week. The payment proposal shall be prepared on Monday and released on Wednesday.

7.2 The Accounts Payable Manager shall prepare the payment proposal. The Corporate Controller shall review and approve the proposal prior to file generation. Evidence of approval shall be retained.

7.3 Automated clearing house payment files shall be transmitted to the Company's depository institution through the institution's commercial banking portal. The individual transmitting the file shall not be the individual releasing the file.

7.4 Wire transfers shall be initiated only upon receipt of a completed and signed Wire Transfer Request Form. Wire transfers shall be subject to dual authorization within the banking portal without regard to value.

7.5 Manual checks shall be issued only in exceptional circumstances and only with the written authorization of the Corporate Controller. The check signature plate shall be maintained under lock and key.

7.6 The Company shall transmit a positive pay issue file to its depository institution on each date on which checks are issued. Positive pay exception items shall be dispositioned by the Accounts Payable Manager not later than the deadline established by the depository institution.

---

## 9. SUPPLIER ACCOUNT RECONCILIATION AND MASTER DATA

9.1 The Accounts Payable Specialist shall obtain and reconcile supplier statements of account on a monthly basis for all suppliers with annual spend in excess of fifty thousand dollars ($50,000). Reconciling items shall be documented and cleared within thirty (30) days.

9.2 Reconciliations shall be evidenced by a completed reconciliation worksheet retained on the finance shared drive in the folder designated for that purpose.

9.3 Additions and modifications to the supplier master record shall be performed only by personnel holding the Vendor Maintenance role. Personnel holding the Vendor Maintenance role shall not hold payment preparation, payment approval, or banking portal entitlements.

9.4 Requests to modify supplier remittance banking details shall be verified by telephone callback to a contact number obtained from the supplier master record and not from the request. The verification shall be documented on the supplier record. The callback shall be performed by an Accounts Payable Specialist.

9.5 A review of the supplier master file for inactive, duplicate and incomplete records shall be performed semi-annually by the Accounts Payable Manager and the results reported to the Corporate Controller.
