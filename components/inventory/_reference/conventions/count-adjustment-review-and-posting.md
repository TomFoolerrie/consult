# count-adjustment-review-and-posting — phrasing decisions

- Weekly cadence phrased as "review on Friday, posting on Monday".
- The signed artifact is "the signed adjustment log" (the Plant Controller "signs the printed log").
- Rejected lines "go back for a third count" (ordinal: third count, after the recount).
- The weekend lag phrased as "approved-but-unposted adjustments".
